# MentorMAMA — Product Problem

## Problem

Midwifery students in clinical placement do not consistently receive structured,
safe mentorship. Ward conditions, mentor availability, and high student-to-mentor
ratios make induction, teaching interactions, and concerns difficult to record and
follow up consistently.

## Intended outcome

MentorMAMA gives students, mentors, ward managers, and university coordinators a
shared workflow for safe placement setup, induction, mentorship evidence, and
student voice without collecting patient-identifiable information.

## Primary user outcome

A clinical mentor can complete a useful mentorship session log in three minutes or
less, including when connectivity is unreliable, with no silent loss of work.

## Scope authority

The Developer Concept Note remains authoritative for programme intent and MVP
scope. The confirmed behaviour and constraints are defined in the Stage 3–8
artifacts and the document reconciliation record.
