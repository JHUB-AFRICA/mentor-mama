# Account enhancements

## Notification preferences

Users can manage operational email categories from Account & Security →
Notifications: account activity, programme updates, placement updates, and a
weekly digest. Security alerts remain enabled and are not user-disableable.
Preferences are stored per account and updated immediately with inline saving
feedback.

## Access policy review

Programme Admins can review the server-owned role matrix from Programme Settings
→ Access policy. Each role shows its scope, MFA requirement, and capabilities.
The pilot deliberately keeps role and organisation-scope changes out of this
screen; invitations and approved support actions remain the controlled paths.

## Sessions and devices

Account Security now shows session device, last-seen time, expiry, and IP data.
Users can refresh the inventory, revoke an individual other session through a
confirmation dialog, revoke all other sessions, or sign out of the current
session. Empty and loading states are explicit.
